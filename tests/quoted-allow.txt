# Quoted texts that no corpus case pins as a whole text (tests/README.md,
# "Quoted texts"). Each entry covers one document. It is the text, " # ",
# the document, with ":" and the lines that the entry covers where the
# document quotes the text unpinned on more than one line, and then one of
# these:
#
# - " = ", and the cases that show the text in the role that the sentence
#   gives it. Each case is its id after its role. A role is the rule whose
#   node spans exactly the text in the case's tree, `words` for words that
#   some stage gives as the labels of tokens in a row, one label for each
#   word, where the tokens stand together in the case's text, or `reject`
#   for a text that the dialect rejects at the text or the word after it.
#   A role applies to the ids after it. The check parses each case and
#   requires a case of each dialect that the text needs. Use this form
#   for a part of a text that the sentence makes a claim about: a special
#   grouping, a rejection or a repair.
# - " # " and why the text needs no case. It is notation, or a shape that a
#   rule produces as written, or a part of a reading that the grammar does
#   not choose, where the whole text has its own case. A reason that begins
#   with "deferred:" names the branch that owes the text a pin.
#
# node tools/quoted-texts.js checks it.
